WHAT IS THE FEE RESERVE?
The fee reserve is a small USDC buffer you deposit upfront. Quill Bot deducts performance fees from it automatically after winning trades — no manual payments needed.
Only deducted when you profit. Losing trades cost zero in fees — you only pay when the bot makes you money.
Five tiers, different minimums:
🟤 Bronze — Flat $5 USDC minimum reserve
⚪ Silver — Flat $10 USDC minimum reserve
🟡 Gold — 2% of active position size
🔵 Platinum — 2% of active position size
💎 Diamond — 2% of active position size
Gold, Platinum, and Diamond tiers require a minimum weekly trading volume to maintain their status. Missing the volume threshold triggers a grace period before demotion.
Your funds stay safe. You can withdraw your remaining reserve at any time — it's always your money.
