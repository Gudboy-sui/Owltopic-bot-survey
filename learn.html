Four Steps to a Trade

Every trade Quill executes passes through the same rigorous four-step pipeline — no shortcuts, no guesswork.

01
📡

Market Scan

The bot continuously scans major crypto futures pairs using live exchange data. Price action, volume, and order book depth are monitored in real time.

02
📊

Signal Analysis

A suite of proprietary technical signals and sentiment data analyse the market simultaneously. Each signal contributes to the overall picture before any decision is made.

03

AI Consensus

Multiple AI models each vote BUY, SELL, or HOLD based on the signal data. A clear majority must agree before any trade is confirmed — disagreement means no trade.

Once consensus is reached, the bot executes the trade on your connected futures account. Dynamic stop-loss and take-profit levels are set automatically based on current market volatility.

◈ Pricing

How Fees Work

Quill only charges a performance fee — and only when a trade is profitable. If the bot loses, you pay zero. The fee is a flat 10%, the same for every user — no tiers, no discounts. What varies is which balance the fee comes out of.

①
Reward Credit Balance — drawn first
Free credit from referrals, milestones, or admin giveaways. Spend-only — never withdrawable. Expires 30 days after being added, so Quill always uses this before touching your own money.
②
Gift Card Balance — drawn second
Added when you redeem a Quill Gift Card. Spend-only — never withdrawable. Used only once your Reward Credit is exhausted.
③
Money Balance — drawn last
Your real USDC deposits. Withdrawable at any time. Only touched once both balances above are empty — your own funds are always protected first.

◈ Capital Preservation

A Bot That Knows When Not To Trade

Most bots are built to trade constantly. Quill is built to trade correctly — which sometimes means doing nothing at all. Two core safeguards exist for one reason: to protect your capital before chasing profits.

🛑

Consecutive Loss Circuit Breaker

If the bot takes a defined number of losses in a row, it stops trading entirely and waits. No revenge trades. No chasing losses. The market gets space to stabilise before Quill re-engages — on its terms, not the market's.

🧭

Regime Detection

Quill reads the current market environment before every session — trending, ranging, volatile, or thin. If conditions don't match a tradeable regime, the bot sits out completely. You don't earn by trading badly; you earn by only trading well.

⏱️

Time-Based Filtering

Not all hours are equal. Quill avoids low-liquidity windows and historically unreliable time slots. Entering a trade at the wrong hour is a risk no signal can fix — so Quill doesn't take that risk.

🔒

Per-Coin Cooldowns

After closing a position on any asset, Quill enforces a cooldown before re-entering that same coin. This prevents compounding losses on a single pair during erratic moves — a discipline most human traders can't maintain.

The goal is not to win every trade — it is to still be in the game when the high-quality setups arrive.

◈ Glossary

Terms You Should Know

Futures
A contract to buy or sell an asset at a future price. Unlike spot trading, futures allow leverage — meaning you can control a larger position with less capital. Quill trades perpetual futures.
Leverage
A multiplier on your position size. Higher leverage means greater exposure — both gains and losses are amplified. Quill adjusts leverage automatically based on your chosen personality mode.
Stop-Loss
A price level that automatically closes a losing trade to cap downside. Quill sets stop-losses dynamically, calibrated to current market conditions.
Take-Profit
A price level that automatically closes a winning trade to lock in gains. Quill uses a staged take-profit approach — securing part of the position early while letting the rest run.
Fee Reserve
A USDC buffer you deposit upfront — separate from your trading capital on the exchange. Quill deducts a flat 10% performance fee from it automatically after winning trades, drawing from Reward Credit first, then Gift Card, then Money Balance last. Only Money Balance and Referral Earnings can be withdrawn. Topped up by you when low.
R:R Ratio
Risk-to-Reward ratio. Quill requires a minimum R:R threshold before entering any trade — ensuring only high-quality setups with favourable reward potential are taken.
