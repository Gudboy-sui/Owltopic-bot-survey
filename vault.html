WHAT IS THE FEE RESERVE?
The fee reserve is a small USDC buffer you deposit upfront. Quill Bot deducts performance fees from it automatically after winning trades — no manual payments needed.
Only deducted when you profit. Losing trades cost zero in fees — you only pay when the bot makes you money.
One flat fee for everyone: 10%. No tiers, no discounts based on volume or status.
Your reserve has three balances, and fees are drawn from them in this order: Reward Credit first, then Gift Card, then Money last. This way your free and bonus funds always get used before your own deposits.
Only your Money Balance and Referral Earnings can be withdrawn. Gift Card and Reward Credit balances are spend-only — they go toward fees, never to your wallet.
